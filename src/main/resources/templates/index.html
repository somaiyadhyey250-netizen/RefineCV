Two evaluation modes tailored to your job search

General Review

A comprehensive diagnostic review of your resume's overall strength, skill coverage, ATS readiness, and formatting clarity.

  • Overall score out of 100 with milestone ruler
  • Strongest skills identified vs notable skill gaps
  • Strengths and weaknesses audit
  • Prioritized edit checklist

Specific Job Match

Target your resume directly against an open role description or industry context (such as "banking sector" or "Java Developer").

  • Job match percentage and role fit score
  • Keyword alignment against job requirements
  • Experience and scope alignment
  • Targeted skill recommendations for that position
